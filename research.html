Research

Thermal sciences, Computational fluid dynamics, and sustainable energy and materials.

I started in naval architecture, which is mechanical engineering applied to ships. The parts I kept wanting more of were fluid flow, heat transfer, and energy conversion. My capstone was the design of a 148 TEU container vessel. I carried one set of numbers from principal particulars through hydrostatics, stability, Holtrop resistance, propeller design, and engine selection. An early assumption changed almost everything after it.

Engine selection made me curious about fuel systems, and that became my thesis, published at MARTEC 2024. I compared six alternative fuel systems against diesel for an inland tanker in Bangladesh. The study used life cycle assessment and life cycle cost analysis over a 25-year service life. It worked at the level of the whole system, so the engine appeared only as a set of numbers. By the end, I had questions it could not answer. What happens inside the combustion chamber? How does heat move through the exchangers around it? I want to spend my Ph.D. on these questions, using CFD, conjugate heat transfer and combustion modelling. I have used COMSOL, Abaqus, MATLAB, Python and C++ in practical coursework, and I want to build on that.

My projects at IBA taught me to check results before trusting them. In our mobile money paper, re-running the R analysis in Python exposed two errors. The corrected result was weaker than the draft, so we rebuilt the paper around it. In the app review study, I compared the sentiment model against a hand-labelled set and against a second model. I reported where they disagreed instead of choosing the friendlier number. In simulation, the same checks are mesh studies, comparison with other codes, and comparison with experimental data. I also like working with large amounts of data, and I expect that to help in CFD, where one study can produce hundreds of runs to sort through and optimize.

I am most drawn to groups working on multiphase flow, thermal management, and clean combustion or energy conversion. I am also open to additive manufacturing, materials for energy systems, and the control of energy systems.
