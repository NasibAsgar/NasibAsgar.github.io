Research

Computational fluid dynamics, thermal sciences, and sustainable energy.

My research interest sits where computational fluid dynamics, thermal sciences, and sustainable energy systems meet. Naval architecture gave me mechanics applied to large structures moving through water, where thermal, structural, and economic constraints all apply at once.

My undergraduate thesis, published at MARTEC 2024, was a well-to-wake life cycle assessment of alternative fuels for Bangladesh's inland fleet. Its central lesson was that fuel rankings do not transfer between places: local supply-chain conditions reorder the carbon footprints, so a decarbonisation decision needs an analysis built for the local system. In a Ph.D. I want to work at smaller scales and higher fidelity, coupling CFD, conjugate heat transfer, and combustion modelling to design efficient thermal and energy-conversion systems.

I have taken a research question from framing through modelling to a peer-reviewed paper. My working tools are COMSOL, Abaqus, MATLAB, Python, and C++, which I have applied to heat transfer, FEA, hydrostatics, and data-analysis problems.

Two group projects at IBA taught me the habit I most want to bring to simulation work: check a result against an independent calculation before trusting it. In the mobile-money paper, re-running the R code in Python exposed two errors and the corrected result was weaker, so we rebuilt the paper around it. In the review-analysis project I compared the sentiment model with a hand-labelled set and with a second model, and reported where they disagreed. In computational mechanics the same roles belong to code-to-code comparison, mesh studies, and experimental data.

Why mechanical engineering. Naval architecture is mechanical engineering applied to ships, and fluid flow, heat transfer, and energy conversion were the parts I wanted more of. The MBA added statistics and research-methods work, which the two group projects draw on, but the questions I want to spend a doctorate on are physical ones.

I am most drawn to groups working on multiphase flow, thermal management, and clean combustion or energy conversion. I am also open to additive manufacturing and the control of energy systems.
