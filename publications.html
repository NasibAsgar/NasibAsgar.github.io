Life Cycle Assessment of Alternative Marine Fuels for Inland Shipping of Bangladesh

Jannatul Ambia Shorna, Nasib Asgar, N. M. Golam Zakaria

Proceedings of the International Conference on Marine Technology (MARTEC 2024). Peer-reviewed conference paper, from my undergraduate thesis.

A well-to-wake life cycle assessment of candidate alternative fuels for Bangladesh's inland shipping, modelled in SimaPro against environmental-impact and techno-economic criteria, using supply-chain data, emission inventories, and regulatory standards.

Key finding. Fuel rankings do not transfer: local supply-chain conditions change the well-to-wake carbon intensity of each option. The study identifies the pathway with the strongest combined environmental and economic case for the inland fleet.

Methods: life cycle assessment, well-to-wake analysis, SimaPro, techno-economic evaluation, emission inventories.

Digital financial inclusion, institutional quality and income inequality: evidence from 36 developing economies, 2017 to 2022

Md. Iftekharul Amin, K. M. Walid, Nasib Asgar (corresponding author), Alif Bin Hasan, Ittesaf Rahman

Preprint on Research Square. Manuscript prepared for Empirical Economics, not yet submitted.

Does the inequality-reducing effect of mobile money depend on how well a country is governed? We estimated two-way fixed-effects models on a panel of 143 country-years across 36 developing economies. MBA research at IBA, with Dr. Amin as supervising faculty.

Key finding. No average effect (p = 0.68). The interaction with institutional quality is negative in all 36 leave-one-country-out runs but not significant (-0.077, p = 0.15). Entering the six governance indicators separately gives signs that split, while one composite index gives a stable estimate.

My part. I contributed to the analysis, the independent Python replication of the R code, and the manuscript. The replication caught two errors in our first draft, which showed p of about 0.05, and we rewrote the paper around the weaker corrected result.

Methods: two-way fixed effects, clustered standard errors, principal-component indices, R (plm), Python.
