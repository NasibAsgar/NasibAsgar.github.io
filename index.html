Nasib Asgar

I graduated in naval architecture from BUET in 2024, and I am applying for Ph.D. positions in mechanical engineering for Fall 2027. My interests are fluid dynamics, thermal sciences, and sustainable energy and materials.

My undergraduate thesis compared alternative fuels for inland ships in Bangladesh and was published at MARTEC 2024. For my capstone I worked with two classmates on a 148 TEU container vessel for the Dhaka to Chattogram route, from the principal particulars through to engine selection. I would like to work on CFD and heat transfer next.
