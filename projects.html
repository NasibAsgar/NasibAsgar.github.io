Projects

A vessel design, two data-analysis projects, and a shipyard study.

Design of a 148 TEU inland container vessel

Completed Capstone design at BUET. Supervised by Prof. Dr. Md. Mashud Karim.

  • Found the principal particulars by iterative optimisation, balancing cargo capacity, hydrostatic stability, resistance, weight, and installed power.
  • Ran stability and hydrostatic analysis in Maxsurf and estimated resistance and powering requirements.
  • Produced structural and general-arrangement drawings in AutoCAD and Rhinoceros 3D.

Tools: Maxsurf, AutoCAD, Rhinoceros 3D. Topics: hydrostatic stability, resistance and powering.

Measuring what users say: NLP on 110,000 reviews of five Bangladeshi mobile finance apps

Completed Group project, MBA at IBA, University of Dhaka, 2025 to 2026. I built the pipeline and drove the analysis; the report and slides were group deliverables.

A first pass with a sentiment model put 29.8% of 110,721 Google Play reviews (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, TAP) in the negative class. After post-stratification weighting the figure is 17.0%. Most of the work went into checking how far either number can be trusted.

  • Built the pipeline in Python: scraping, script detection, a two-model sentiment cascade, post-stratification weights, aspect tagging, near-duplicate detection, and difference-in-differences on 138 app releases.
  • The two classifiers disagree on 24.1% of English reviews but 57.2% of Bengali and 63.8% of Banglish ones, so the sentiment numbers are weakest where many local users write.
  • Checked the model against a 600-review hand-labelled set (66.5% accurate, from the project report) and logged the open problems, including a gold-set answer key whose IDs did not match, so I back-filled no labels.

Tools and methods: Python, bootstrap intervals, inter-rater agreement, effective sample size, MinHash LSH, difference-in-differences.
