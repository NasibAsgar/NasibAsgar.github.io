Capstone project

Design of a 148 TEU inland container vessel

Completed Ship design project (NAME 338) at BUET, in a group of three. Supervised by Prof. Dr. Md. Mashud Karim.

A 148 TEU vessel at 12 knots for the Pangaon to Chattogram run. The route sets the envelope: 75 m length, 4 m draft, 18.5 m air draft. Everything else follows.

  • 75.62 m overall, 14.943 m breadth, 3.78 m draft, block coefficient 0.69, 2,834 tonnes displacement, reached by iterating against a basis ship.
  • Hydrostatics and stability in Maxsurf. Every IMO intact criterion passes in all four loading conditions.
  • Holtrop resistance of 88.76 kN at 12 knots, giving 548 kW effective power.
  • Twin screw. A four-blade 2.268 m propeller at 290 rpm, clearing the Burrill check at zero cavitation, driven by two Yanmar 6AYEM-GT engines at 749 kW each.
  • Twin NACA-0020 rudders to GL rules, plus scantlings, shafting, engine foundation and the general arrangement in AutoCAD and Rhinoceros 3D.

Tools: Maxsurf, AutoCAD, Rhinoceros 3D. Topics: hydrostatic stability, resistance and powering, propeller design, machinery selection.

Data analysis

Two projects from the MBA at IBA. The pipelines, the models and the checks on them are mine.

Measuring what users say: NLP on 110,000 reviews of five Bangladeshi mobile finance apps

Completed Group project, MBA at IBA, 2025 to 2026. I built the pipeline and drove the analysis.

A sentiment model put 29.8% of 110,721 Google Play reviews in the negative class. Weighting brings that to 17.0%. Most of the work went into asking how far either number can be trusted.

  • Python pipeline: scraping, script detection, a two-model sentiment cascade, post-stratification weights, near-duplicate detection, and difference-in-differences across 138 app releases.
  • The two classifiers disagree on 24.1% of English reviews but 63.8% of Banglish ones, so the numbers are weakest where many local users write.
  • Against a 600-review hand-labelled set the model is 66.5% accurate. The gold set's answer key had mismatched IDs, so I back-filled no labels.

Tools and methods: Python, bootstrap intervals, inter-rater agreement, MinHash LSH, difference-in-differences.

Project summary (PDF)

Tap, pay, overspend? Present bias and unplanned spending on mobile financial services

Completed Buyer Behaviour (M-601), MBA at IBA, August 2026. Group of five, supervised by Dr. Rezwanul Huque Khan.

Across 256 responses, the obvious explanations came out flat: usage intensity (β = 0.079, p = .188), financial prudence (β = -0.069) and financial literacy (p = .444). Present bias held, at β = 0.309 (p < .001).

  • Bilingual survey of young urban users, analysed in Python: factor analysis, regression, moderation and mediation, K-means segmentation, logistic classification.
  • Education was the only demographic split that mattered, and it ran against the intuition: the lowest-education group reported the most unplanned spending.
  • Our composite scales came in below the usual reliability thresholds, which the paper reports rather than leaves out.

Tools and methods: Python, factor analysis, regression, moderation and mediation, K-means clustering, logistic regression.

Simulation

Heat-transfer models I built in COMSOL. Each one rebuilds a published benchmark from the geometry up, then checks the result against the numbers the source reports.

Benchmark natural convection in a differentially heated square cavity

Completed Validated against de Vahl Davis and Saha et al. (2008).

The hot-wall Nusselt number came out at 1.118, 2.245, 4.521 and 8.826 for Ra = 10³ to 10⁶. The non-dimensional model sits within 0.16% of the published benchmark, the dimensional one within 0.34%.

  • Built the cavity twice, once non-dimensionally and once with real air properties driven to matching Rayleigh numbers, to confirm the two formulations land on the same answer.
  • Buoyancy went in as a volume force. Nu came from a boundary integral of the wall temperature gradient, evaluated across all four Rayleigh numbers in one run.
  • A third model swapped the hot wall for a sinusoidal profile, T = sin(2πωx), on a mapped mesh, and swept Prandtl number from 0.7 to 7 and frequency ω from 0.5 to 2.

Tools: COMSOL Multiphysics 6.2 (Laminar Flow, Heat Transfer in Fluids), parametric sweeps, custom Nu integration. Topics: natural convection, mesh refinement, benchmark validation.

Natural convection from a heated strip on a cavity floor

Completed Reproducing Corvaro & Paroncini (2008), numerical and experimental.

  • Modelled a short heater strip on the floor of an air-filled square cavity (Pr = 0.71), the heater one-fifth of the span and centred on the base.
  • Average Nu over the strip reached 5.13, 6.39 and 6.71 at Ra = 7.6×10⁴, 2.0×10⁵ and 2.5×10⁵, three decimals off the reference simulation.
  • The model runs about 5 to 7% above the measured Nu, the same gap the original paper reports between its own simulation and its experiment.

Tools: COMSOL, nonlocal boundary integration for surface Nu. Topics: discrete heating, numerical and experimental comparison.

Mixed, conjugate and MHD convection with internal bodies

Completed Three cavity cases, each checked against its source paper.

  • Vented cavity with two rotating cylinders in Cu-water nanofluid (3% particles, Re = 600). As rotation rose from rest to Rew = 100, the downstream cylinder's Nu went from 17.3 to 22.5, within 0.45% of Shuvro et al. (2023).
  • Lid-driven cavity with a spinning conducting cylinder, solving the solid and the fluid together. Swept Richardson number from 0.1 to 10 at three rotation rates, and Nu followed the Mahmud et al. (2022) curve from 3.4 to 6.5.
  • Square chamber with a heat-generating steel block under a magnetic field and Joule heating (Tasnim et al. 2023). Swept Rayleigh from 10³ to 10⁶, Hartmann number from 0 to 20, and the heat-generation term, with Nu averaged over the block's four faces.

Tools: COMSOL (Laminar Flow, Heat Transfer in Solids and Fluids, volume forces for the buoyancy, Lorentz and Joule terms), multi-parameter sweeps. Topics: conjugate heat transfer, nanofluids, mixed convection, magnetohydrodynamics.
