Capstone

Design of a 148 TEU inland container vessel

Completed Ship design project at BUET, in a group of three.

We designed a 148 TEU container vessel for the Pangaon to Chattogram route, running at 12 knots. The route limits the ship to 75 m in length, 4 m in draft and 18.5 m in air draft.

  • The ship is 75.62 m long overall and 14.943 m wide. Its draft is 3.78 m, its block coefficient is 0.69, and it displaces 2,834 tonnes.
  • We worked out hydrostatics and stability in Maxsurf. The ship passes every IMO intact criterion in all four loading conditions.
  • Holtrop resistance at 12 knots is 88.76 kN, so the effective power is 548 kW.
  • Two Yanmar 6AYEM-GT engines of 749 kW each drive twin four-blade propellers, 2.268 m in diameter. The propeller passes the Burrill check with no cavitation.

Tools: Maxsurf, AutoCAD, Rhinoceros 3D.

Measuring what users say: NLP on 110,000 reviews of five Bangladeshi mobile finance apps

Completed Group project, MBA at IBA. I built the pipeline and led the analysis.

A sentiment model labelled 29.8% of 110,721 Google Play reviews as negative. After post-stratification weighting, the share was 17.0%. Most of the work went into asking how far either number can be trusted.

  • The two classifiers disagree on 24.1% of English reviews and 63.8% of Banglish ones. The numbers are weakest where many local users write.
  • On a 600-review hand-labelled set, the model is 66.5% accurate.
  • The Python pipeline scrapes the reviews, runs a two-model sentiment cascade, finds near-duplicates with MinHash LSH and puts bootstrap intervals on the results. I also ran a difference-in-differences analysis across 138 app releases.

Tap, pay, overspend? Present bias and unplanned spending on mobile financial services

Completed MBA project at IBA, group of five.

We surveyed 256 young urban users in two languages. Present bias predicted unplanned spending (β = 0.309, p < .001). Usage intensity (β = 0.079, p = .188) and financial literacy (p = .444) were not significant, and financial prudence was close to zero (β = -0.069).

  • We analysed the data in Python with factor analysis, regression, moderation and mediation, K-means clustering and logistic regression.
  • Our composite scales were below the usual reliability thresholds, and the paper says so.

Simulation

These are heat-transfer models I built in COMSOL. For each one I rebuilt a published benchmark and compared my results with the numbers in the source.

Natural convection in a differentially heated square cavity

Completed Validated against de Vahl Davis and Saha et al. (2008).

The hot-wall Nusselt number was 1.118, 2.245, 4.521 and 8.826 for Ra = 10³ to 10⁶. My non-dimensional model is within 0.16% of the benchmark and the dimensional one is within 0.34%.

  • I built the cavity twice, once non-dimensionally and once with real air properties at matching Rayleigh numbers, to see whether the two agree.

Natural convection from a heated strip on a cavity floor

Completed Reproduces Corvaro & Paroncini (2008), numerical and experimental.

  • The model has a short heater strip on the floor of an air-filled square cavity (Pr = 0.71). The strip is one-fifth of the span and centred on the base.
  • Average Nu over the strip was 5.13, 6.39 and 6.71 at Ra = 7.6×10⁴, 2.0×10⁵ and 2.5×10⁵. These differ from the reference simulation only in the third decimal.
  • My Nu is 5 to 7% above the measured values. The original paper reports the same gap between its own simulation and its experiment.

Mixed, conjugate and MHD convection with internal bodies

Completed Three cavity cases, each checked against its source paper.

  • A vented cavity with two rotating cylinders in Cu-water nanofluid (3% particles, Re = 600). As rotation went from rest to Rew = 100, the downstream cylinder's Nu rose from 17.3 to 22.5, within 0.45% of the published benchmark.
  • A lid-driven cavity with a spinning conducting cylinder, with solid and fluid solved together. Nu followed the Mahmud et al. (2022) curve from 3.4 to 6.5 as Richardson number went from 0.1 to 10.
  • A square chamber with a heat-generating steel block under a magnetic field and Joule heating (Tasnim et al. 2023). I swept Rayleigh number from 10³ to 10⁶ and Hartmann number from 0 to 20, and averaged Nu over the block's four faces.
